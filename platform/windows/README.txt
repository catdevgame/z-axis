@APP_NAME@ @APP_VERSION@ for Windows x64: native ports of PlayStation 2 games,
statically recompiled with PS2Recomp. It holds no game data: at first start it asks for each game's PS2 disc
(an ISO, a BIN/CUE dump or the disc's folder) and copies its files into
%APPDATA%\@APP_NAME@.

Settings, controls, saves, mods and logs: %APPDATA%\@APP_NAME@
Full screen: Alt+Enter or F11. Settings: the game's own menus
(Options > Settings, or Settings in the pause menu).
